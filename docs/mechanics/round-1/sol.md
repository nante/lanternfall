# Round 1 — mechanics research (Sol)

Read-only audit, 2026-11-09. Paths below are relative to the project; `engine.ts`, `model.ts`, `rules.ts`, `world.ts`, and `content-schema.ts` mean `packages/game/src/…`; `main.ts` and `render/view.ts` mean `apps/client/src/…`. **TOP 10** marks the recommended implementation priorities.

Target is **post–12 December 2005, version 7.6**, not generic “old Tibia.” [CipSoft's announcement](https://www.tibia.com/news/?id=355&subtopic=newsarchive) specifically changed mana regeneration and spell level requirements. Tibiantis documents 7.4; Nostalrius/OTHire reconstruct 7.7/7.72 and contain customizations and bugs. Their agreement is evidence, not proof of an exact 7.6 constant. All fixes below concern independently implemented rules and original content; no assets, maps, dialogue, names or content datasets should be imported.

1. **TOP 10 — Movement speed and diagonal timing.** **7.6:** classic speed units `S = 218 + 2L`; step time approximately `ceil((1000 × groundSpeed / S × diagonalCost)/50) × 50 ms`, diagonalCost `3`, cardinal `1`. Typical terrain factors: paved/wood `100`, dirt `110`, rock `120`, grass `150`, sand `160`, snow `200`; tile variants differ. **Code:** `engine.ts:431` uses fixed `300 ms × 1.45` diagonally; `world.ts:44` prices diagonals at `2.4`, ignores ground speed. **Fix:** share a terrain/level step-cost function between scheduling and pathfinding; preserve 50 ms timing resolution. **Confidence: medium** (high on level/terrain dependency and 3× diagonals; rounding order differs among reconstructions). [Classic speed research](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/speed), [7.7 reconstruction](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/creature.cpp#L1326).

2. **TOP 10 — Physical damage and attack modes.** **7.6 reconstruction:** set effective attack `A' = A + floor(0.2A)` offensive, `A` balanced, `A - floor(0.4A)` defensive; raw hit `ceil((5s+50) × A' × floor((U+V)/2)/10000)`, independent `U,V ∈ [0,99]`. Maximum uses random factor `99`; **no additive level term** in this reconstruction. **Code:** `engine.ts:446` uses `floor(A × (0.6+s/18)+L)`, uniform random hit, and has no stance. **Fix:** replace the level-heavy formula with the historical triangular roll and expose three modes; do not substitute modern `0.085 × skill × attack + level/5`. **Confidence: medium** (7.4 research and 7.7 reconstruction agree broadly, integer details need parity checks). [Combat implementation](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/combat.cpp#L796), [classic combat research](https://tibiantis.online/?id=109&p=1&page=viewtopic).

3. **TOP 10 — Shield defense, armor and limited blocking.** **7.6:** shield/weapon defense is rolled before armor; approximately `(5s+50) × (D+1) × stance × triangularRoll/10000`, defense stance factors offensive/balanced/defensive `0.6/1/1.8` (integer adjustment precedes roll). Without shield, weapon defense and its skill can defend. For total armor `a ≥ 2`, reduction is `floor(a/2) + U[0,floor(a/2)-1]`; armor still works after shield defense is exhausted. Classic shielding handles roughly **two blockable attacks per 2-second turn**, not arbitrary attackers. **Code:** `engine.ts:509–511` uses uniform `D × (0.5+s/20)`, no weapon fallback or block budget, then uniform `0…armor`; outgoing hits have the same armor problem at `:447`. **Fix:** implement separate defense/armor stages, capped defense opportunities, and distinguish their hit effects. **Confidence: medium** (high on missing block cap/armor shape; exact refill/window behavior differs between emulators). [Defense/armor source](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/player.cpp#L188), [block budget reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/OTHire/blob/master/source/creature.cpp#L1050).

4. **TOP 10 — Attack interval.** **7.6:** ordinary melee swings every **2000 ms**, including misses; stance changes damage, not cadence. **Code:** `content/balance.json:15` is `1500`; applied at `engine.ts:449`. Monster base attack interval is already `2000` (`balance.json:16`). **Fix:** set player base attack interval to 2000 ms and retain one timer across target changes. **Confidence: high.** [Historical-vocation reconstruction](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/data/XML/vocations.xml).

5. **TOP 10 — XP must be conserved between attackers.** **7.6:** attackers receive damage-proportional XP, `floor(monsterXP × creditedDamage / totalCreditedDamage)` at 1× rate; party equal-share is a later feature. **Code:** `engine.ts:385` gives the highest-damage player **all XP** and every other attacker another `ceil(XP/3)`; `:369` also credits uncapped overkill. **Fix:** cap credited damage to HP actually removed and distribute one XP pool by contribution; do not add party bonus/equal sharing. **Confidence: high** on proportionality; **medium** on historical overkill/accounting edge cases. [Damage-ratio source](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/OTHire/blob/master/source/creature.cpp#L1235).

6. **TOP 10 — Death is substantially too forgiving.** **7.6 baseline:** unpromoted/unblessed death loses **10% total XP, accumulated skill tries and mana spent**; backpack contents drop, each other equipped item has **10% loss chance**. Promotion/blessings are separate optional mechanics, not baseline discounts. **Code:** `engine.ts:393–398`, `content/balance.json:18–19`: pack drops, XP loses 8%, melee/shield lose 5%, magic loses nothing, other equipment never drops. **Fix:** apply baseline loss consistently to all progression totals and roll remaining equipment independently; keep original names for any future protection mechanics. **Confidence: medium** (historical baseline well corroborated; low-level exceptions need separate validation). [Pre-8.41 distinction](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Death), [7.6 recreation's explicit baseline](https://www.oldcores.com/wiki/game-mechanics/death-penalty), [contemporary archived manual mirror](https://tibiaclub.szm.sk/tibiapvp.html).

7. **TOP 10 — Food and regeneration.** **7.6 no-vocation reference:** **+1 HP per 6 s**, **+1 mana per 6 s**, while fed; no level-based scaling. Food budget caps at **1200 s**, rejecting a meal that would exceed it. Post-7.6 vocation-specific mana rates must not be inferred from 7.4. **Code:** `engine.ts:414` gives `1+floor(L/4)` HP and `2+floor(L/3)` mana every 3 s; `:319` checks existing food against 1500 s, then adds the whole meal, allowing overshoot. **Fix:** separate HP/mana clocks and flat amounts; check `fed + meal <= 1_200_000` before consuming. **Confidence: medium** for no-vocation mana tick (OTHire differs); high on level scaling/fullness mismatch. [No-vocation reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/data/XML/vocations.xml#L3), [food limit](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Food).

8. **TOP 10 — Separate skills and restore progression constants.** **7.6:** sword/axe/club/fist start at `10`; next-level tries `50 × b^(s−10)`, shielding `100 × b^(s−10)`; ML starts at `0`, next level costs `1600 × b^ML` **mana spent**. Bases are vocation-independent; growth **is not**. No-vocation reference: melee `b=2`, fist/shield `b=1.5`, magic `b=4`. **Code:** `model.ts:34` has one melee total; `rules.ts:11–30`, `balance.json:23–27` use bases `40/30/60`, shared `1.12`, magic `1.35`. Fists train the same melee stat (`engine.ts:444–450`). **Fix:** separate four weapon skills and shielding; preserve total-tries storage, replace bases and select an explicit growth profile. A custom shared profile is a design deviation, not a historical constant. **Confidence: high** on bases/separation; medium on exact no-vocation profile. [Requirement formulas](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/OTHire/blob/master/source/vocation.cpp#L261), [no-vocation multipliers](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/data/XML/vocations.xml#L3).

9. **TOP 10 — Core mouse actions / Use with / Look.** **7.6:** normal controls left-click walk, right-click context menu; classic controls right-click use/attack and Ctrl-click menu. Look is explicit; targeted Use with enters a crosshair then selects a target. **Code:** `main.ts:258–276` makes either click attack/talk/open; `render/view.ts:147` suppresses context menus. `model.ts:47–61` has no Look or Use-with command. **Fix:** add action-specific menus and target selection, server-side reach checks, and independently written look descriptions. Keep “use” distinct from “pick up/equip.” **Confidence: high** on missing actions; medium on exact classic shortcut mapping across old clients. [Controls manual](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual), [legacy options documentation](https://tibia.pl/strona/manual-opcje-gry).

10. **TOP 10 — Protection zones and logout block.** **7.6:** temples/designated houses are PZ: neither receive nor initiate attacks; natural HP/mana regeneration stops, food time continues. Ordinary in-fight logout block is **60 s** after aggression/being attacked, even blocked hits; PvE combat alone does not prohibit entering a PZ. **Code:** `model.ts:23–26` has no tile-zone metadata; `engine.ts:449,512` use 30 s, `:308` accepts logout unconditionally. `:189` correctly keeps a disconnected character present while combat timer lasts. **Fix:** add original-map PZ flags enforced by movement/combat/regen; reject voluntary logout during block, retain disconnect presence. **Confidence: high** on missing PZ; medium on precise 7.6 timeout. [Combat manual](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=combat&subtopic=manual), [7.x reconstruction config](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/config.lua#L5).

11. **Chase, stand, and following players.** **7.6:** attack target, chase/stand toggle, and non-attacking Follow are distinct. Stand continues attacking when in range without chasing; Follow ends when its target leaves the screen. **Code:** `engine.ts:419–422` always chases; `:261–263` cancels attack on manual walking; `:271` only accepts monsters. **Fix:** retain attack target on manual movement; model chase/stand independently and add a separate follow target/cancel action. **Confidence: high.** [Controls manual](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

12. **Skill tries require meaningful combat, not endless blocked swings.** **7.x reference:** a damaging hit grants a melee try and enables the next **30 blocked attempts** to train; further blocked attempts stop training until blood is drawn again. Shielding tries require blockable incoming attacks and a shield, with limited defense opportunities; this is not “only if HP damage occurs.” **Code:** `engine.ts:450` grants every swing forever; `:510` grants shielding on every attacker indefinitely. **Fix:** track the blood-hit training allowance and defense eligibility/budget; exclude immune/non-blockable effects. **Confidence: medium:** 30-hit behavior exists in both reference servers, but shielding credit/window quirks need historical validation. [Skill event source](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/OTHire/blob/master/source/player.cpp#L2233).

13. **No-vocation level gains/capacity baseline.** **7.x reference:** level 1 `150 HP / 0 mana / 400 oz`; per level **+5 HP, +5 mana, +10 oz**, plus +2 speed. Level 8 therefore `185/35/470`. Level-up adds gained HP/mana, not a full refill. **Code:** `balance.json:3–8` starts `150/30/220`, gains `15/10/20`; `rules.ts:22–24` applies these at every level. `engine.ts:210–211` already adds only the increase. **Fix:** correct baseline/gains and movement speed; retain additive restoration. **Confidence: medium** (older emulator configs disagree; the cited source and capacity documentation agree). [Stat handling](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/player.cpp#L1319), [capacity](https://www.tibia-wiki.net/wiki/Capacity), [vocation gains](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/data/XML/vocations.xml#L3).

14. **Spell/fluid exhaustion should have explicit rules.** **7.x reconstruction:** shared spell exhaustion defaults to **2 s aggressive / 1 s non-aggressive**; extended item actions have their own **1 s** reference throttle. Exact 7.6 fluid interaction remains unverified. **Code:** `engine.ts:321` checks `spellAt−500`, then writes `now+1000`, allowing repeated drinks every **500 ms** and shortening longer spell exhaustion; `:348` uses each spell's arbitrary cooldown. **Fix:** remove timestamp subtraction; represent spell exhaustion and fluid/item throttle explicitly. Validate the proposed defaults against a 7.6 recording before treating them as exact. **Confidence: low** for era-specific cooldown matrix, high on the current accidental bypass. [Spell source](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/spells.cpp#L718), [item timing config](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/config.lua).

15. **Doors, stairs, holes and ladders are different actions.** **7.6:** closed ordinary doors require Use; stairs/open holes activate on stepping; ladders require Use; digging/rope-style transitions require a tool used on a target. **Code:** `engine.ts:426–433` automatically opens doors and activates every map link; `:280–282` also activates any linked tile through Use. `model.ts:13` treats hatch/ladder as walkable. **Fix:** put trigger kind on original map links; stop at closed doors and preserve distinct step/use/tool transitions. **Confidence: high** on semantic distinction. [Movement/interaction manual](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

16. **Auto-walk to use must actually execute the deferred action.** **7.x:** a remote reachable Use walks to adjacent range **1**, then retries the chosen action. **Code:** `engine.ts:276–278` sets a path and returns without storing the action; a distant door happens to open through auto-open, but well/vault actions are never retried server-side. `main.ts:327–333` only supplies some client-specific pending actions. **Fix:** retain one cancellable deferred use, execute after reaching its required range, and revalidate object/target. **Confidence: high** on missing continuation. [Use-action reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/game.cpp#L1840).

17. **Numpad, facing and Escape.** **7.6:** cardinal arrows; numpad `7/9/1/3` for diagonals, Ctrl+direction turns without stepping; Escape stops walking/attacking/follow. **Code:** two held arrows already combine into a diagonal (`main.ts:342–345`); no numpad/turn action, numeric keys trigger hotbar (`:355`), and Escape can open menu while a route continues (`:359–364`). Client movement also imposes fixed `>260 ms` send timing. **Fix:** handle physical numpad codes with NumLock off, add turn, send stop first on Escape, schedule keyboard walking against authoritative movement timing. **Confidence: high.** [Keyboard controls](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

18. **Pushing is absent.** **7.x:** dragging a pushable creature/player moves it **one adjacent tile**, actor within reach **1**, destination checked; it is a delayed action, not walk-through. Nostalrius queues **1000 ms**; other old distributions use 2000 ms, so that delay is not established as exact 7.6. **Code:** `engine.ts:184–187` only blocks occupancy; `model.ts:47–61` has no creature-move action; `main.ts:295` only drags items. **Fix:** add validated drag/push and pushable creature metadata; confirm delay before locking balance. **Confidence: medium** on movement rules, low on exact delay. [Push reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/game.cpp#L600).

19. **Map-click has too much world knowledge; exact legacy cap unresolved.** **7.x:** client auto-walk follows known map geometry on the same floor; protocol route length is an unsigned byte (**≤255 directions per message**, not proof of a 255-tile user-visible path limit). **Code:** `engine.ts:265` asks omniscient server A*; `world.ts:23` permits 4000 explored nodes, with no knowledge restriction; closed doors count as passable. **Fix:** constrain destinations/routing to discovered geometry, use terrain costs, cancel at action-only obstacles. Do **not** assert a 100/127-step 7.6 limit without client evidence. **Confidence: low** on exact original path limits; high on code behavior. [Protocol reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/protocolgame.cpp#L656).

20. **Tile stacks are ordered targets, not loot browsers.** **7.x:** ground → ordered fixed decorations → creatures → movable/down-item layers; Look/Use/Move select different eligible top things. Only the exposed movable item is ordinarily dragged; creatures do not categorically prevent a small item landing on their tile. **Code:** `main.ts:269,594,619` opens a whole ground-pile list, exposing buried items; `render/view.ts:272–278` chooses actor/corpse before item semantics. `engine.ts:137–149` already permits items onto occupied eligible ground. **Fix:** centralize action-specific tile selection and top-item eligibility; preserve existing occupied-ground throwing. **Confidence: medium.** [Tile order/visible-target source](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/tile.cpp#L308).

21. **Ground containers partly work, but use is conflated with looting.** **7.6:** Use opens a reachable container at range **1**, leaving it on the floor; its inventory can be manipulated subject to capacity. **Code:** server reach checks support nested ground containers (`engine.ts:55–74`), and `main.ts:597–601` renders their contents automatically inside the pile panel; `:499` makes a ground-container click pick it up instead of open. **Fix:** explicit Open on the selected container, independent windows/current container identity, close access when out of reach. **Confidence: high** on behavior gap. [Container/use manual](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

22. **Monster retargeting, retreat and speed.** **7.x:** species configure target-switch interval/chance and flee-below-HP threshold; not all species flee or switch alike. Movement uses creature speed and terrain, not a universal milliseconds-per-species delay. **Code:** `engine.ts:501–503` retains a valid target indefinitely, otherwise picks nearest; `:505–520` always approaches/attacks regardless of HP and schedules fixed `def.step` with no diagonal surcharge. `content-schema.ts:56–61` has no flee/switch settings. **Fix:** add original species-specific switch/retreat behavior and use shared movement timing; validate values per original creature design rather than importing a monster table. **Confidence: high** on missing behavior; medium on historic scheduler details. [Monster reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/src/monster.cpp#L920), [speed research](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/speed).

23. **Item hotkeys are later than 7.6.** **7.6:** text/spell hotkeys existed; native item-use/self-target hotkeys arrived in **7.8**. **Code:** `main.ts:394–397,355` gives food/healing/mana one-key use from anywhere carried. **Fix:** if strict input parity is intended, retain spell-text shortcuts but require selecting consumables/Use-with; otherwise record this as an intentional convenience deviation. **Confidence: high.** [7.8 change history](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Updates/7.8).

24. **Light has no gameplay resource.** **7.6:** personal light sources/spells have bounded light and duration; underground visibility depends on them, rather than granting permanent personal illumination. **Code:** `render/view.ts:228` gives unconditional cave torch intensity `3.2`; `model.ts:32–38` has no light state. **Fix:** add original timed light sources and authoritative expiry; exact historical radius/duration is **not verified** here. **Confidence: medium** on missing mechanic, low on numeric fidelity. [7.6 colored-light announcement](https://www.tibia.com/news/?id=355&subtopic=newsarchive).

25. **PvP, one line:** 7.6 had white-skull aggression and red-skull repeated unlawful-kill consequences; `engine.ts:271` only targets monsters—add these only if PvP enters scope; **confidence medium**; [7.x skull reference](https://github.com/Ezzz-dev/Nostalrius/blob/master/config.lua#L12).

26. **Keep the existing level XP curve.** **7.6:** total XP at level `L` is `(50L³−300L²+850L−600)/3`, equivalently `(50(L−1)³−150(L−1)²+400(L−1))/3`; levels `2/3/8` require `100/200/4200`. **Code:** `rules.ts:10`, `content/balance.json:9–13` already match exactly. **Fix:** none; retain while correcting kill attribution. Also retain existing adjacent pickup, 100-item stack limit, and throwing onto occupied eligible tiles; do not reimplement the recently added lure/respawn/decay features in this round. **Confidence: high** on XP curve. [Formula reference](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Formulae).

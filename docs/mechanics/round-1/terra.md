# Lanternfall: mechanics differences, round 1

Prioritized for player impact. **★ = top 10.** Confidence is in the historical rule, not the code reading. Tibiantis describes **7.4**, and OT servers are implementations or reconstructions, not proof of CipSoft 7.6; where used below, the 7.6 extrapolation is explicitly qualified. The December 2005 update changed mana regeneration and magic-level requirements. No game content needs to be copied.

1. **★ Movement speed and terrain.** In the 7.4 reconstruction, speed = `218 + 2 × level`; step seconds = `ceil_to_0.05(terrain / speed × diagonalFactor)`, with diagonal factor 3, straight 1. Representative terrain factors: floor 100, grass 150, sand 160, snow 200; level 1 on floor takes 0.50 s straight, 1.40 s diagonal. **Ours:** fixed 300 ms and 1.45× diagonal, independent of level/terrain (`content/balance.json:14`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:424`, `packages/game/src/world.ts:44`); client animation uses fixed speed (`apps/client/src/render/view.ts:423`). **Fix:** store terrain factors and speed, calculate destination-tile duration in 50 ms units, and match path costs and animation to it. **Confidence: medium** (7.4 evidence, 7.6 rounding needs confirmation). [Source](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/speed).

2. **★ Melee timing and damage.** Old combat attacks every **2 s**. A 7.4 reconstruction uses modified weapon attack `A`, skill `S`, and two independent integers 0–99: `floor((5S+50) × A × floor((r1+r2)/2) / 10000)`; maximum uses 99, approximately `(5S+50) × A / 100`. This old formula has **no level term**; confirm it survived 7.6 before adopting it. **Ours:** 1.5 s, uniform `0..floor(attack×(0.6+skill/18)+level)` less uniform armor (`content/balance.json:15`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:442`). **Fix:** use 2 s and the old two-roll distribution, with independent per-weapon skill and stance-adjusted attack. **Confidence: medium** (7.4 reconstruction, 7.6 version boundary). [Calculator and executable formula](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/Physical_Damage_Calculator_2), [old-formula discussion](https://otland.net/threads/otx-2-9-8-6-need-weapondamage-oldschool-7-4.276114/).

3. **★ Defense, armor, and crowding.** A 7.4 reconstruction rolls blocking using the same `(5S+50) × D / 100` scale and two-roll distribution, then armor reduces approximately 50–100% of total armor (calculator: `floor(armor/2) + floor(armor/2 × r/99)`, `r=0..99`, with armor 1–2 special-cased). Shield/weapon defense only applies to the first **two blockable attacks per 2 s turn**; further attacks still face armor. **Ours:** each monster gets unlimited uniform shield and armor reductions, with no monster defense versus player melee (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:447`, `:505`, `:509`). **Fix:** model blockable hit budget, defender skill/defense and armor in that order, including monster defense and weapon defense without shield. **Confidence: medium** (7.4 calculator; exact 7.6 rounding requires confirmation). [Calculator](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/Physical_Damage_Calculator_2), [two-hit limit](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/training).

4. **★ Fighting modes and chase.** The 7.4 reconstruction gives offensive/balanced/defensive attack multipliers **1.2/1.0/0.6** and defense **0.6/1.0/1.8**, with integer rounding; stand versus chase is a separate switch. **Ours:** no stance state; targeting always paths into melee and `walk`/`path` clear target (`packages/game/src/model.ts:32`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:261`, `:419`). **Fix:** persist both mode switches, preserve an attack target while manually moving, and apply stance modifiers. **Confidence: medium** (7.4 stance factors, verify 7.6). [Stances](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/Melee_and_Distance), [movement modes](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

5. **★ Skill identities and advancement.** Sword, axe, club, fist and shielding are separate skills, starting at 10. A standard formula is `base × multiplier^(skill−10)`, with bases 50 melee/fist and 100 shielding; **multipliers depend on vocation**, e.g. 1.1 for knight melee/shield, so a single 1.1 is a suggested vocation-free design choice, not a universal 7.6 constant. **Ours:** one melee counter for every weapon/fist; bases 40/30 and shared growth 1.12 (`packages/game/src/model.ts:34`, `packages/game/src/rules.ts:11`, `content/balance.json:23`). **Fix:** track each skill's total tries separately and choose/document a common multiplier for this vocation-free game. **Confidence: medium** (formula well documented; exact 7.6 rounding and chosen multiplier need validation). [Skill formula and bases](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Formulae), [classic skill multipliers](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/Skills).

6. **★ Skill tries and training.** In the 7.4 reconstruction, an attack attempt can train even if blocked, but continued progress requires at least one damage-dealing hit within **30 eligible attack/block attempts**. A shield trains on eligible blocked attacks, including zero-damage blocks, up to two per combat turn; weapon defense alone does not train shielding. **Ours:** every swing grants melee and every monster attack with a shield grants shielding, indefinitely (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:449`, `:509`). **Fix:** track the blood-hit training window and the per-turn eligible block budget; distinguish attempted, shield-blocked, armor-blocked and damaging hits. **Confidence: medium** (7.4 evidence). [Training](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/training).

7. **★ Death losses.** An old-style unpromoted baseline loses **10% of total XP, accumulated skill tries and spent mana**, backpack and contents, plus an independent **10% chance per other equipped item**; protections can change this. **Ours:** 8% XP, 5% melee/shielding, no magic loss; pack always drops but other equipment never does (`content/balance.json:18`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:388`). **Fix:** apply the baseline to all accumulated skill/magic progress, roll each equipment slot, and put losses into the corpse; add protection mechanics only if desired. **Confidence: medium** (7.6-style server, not official 2005 code). [Source](https://www.oldcores.com/wiki/game-mechanics/death-penalty).

8. **★ Food, regeneration and stats.** A 7.6-style server gives an unclassed player **1 HP and 2 mana per 6 s** while fed; food time caps at **1,200 s**, and nutrition conventionally adds `nutrition × 12 s`. A no-vocation baseline gains **5 HP, 5 mana, 10 capacity per level**; level-1 baseline 150 HP/0 mana/400 cap is a reconstruction, not a requirement to remove Lanternfall's starting spells. **Ours:** every 3 s gains `1+floor(level/4)` HP and `2+floor(level/3)` mana, fullness can exceed 1,500 s, and level gains are 15/10/20 (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:319`, `:412`, `content/balance.json:3`). **Fix:** use independent fixed regen timers, enforce the food cap before consumption, and choose the no-vocation stat baseline deliberately. Level-up currently adds the maximum-stat increase to current HP/mana (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:204`); verify 7.6 current-value behavior separately, as full refill was added later. **Confidence: medium** (server reconstruction and 7.4 food evidence). [7.6-style regen](https://www.oldcores.com/wiki/server-information), [food](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/Food_and_regeneration), [7.6 OT gain defaults](https://tibiaibz.wordpress.com/tibia-ibiza/guias-y-tutoriales/hacer-un-server-desde-0-tibia-76/), [level-up refill history](https://www.tibiaqa.com/9294/when-and-why-did-tibia-start-replenish-our-mana-and-health-after-leveled).

9. **★ Experience distribution.** Without explicit party sharing, experience follows each attacker's damage contribution; for example, 75/25 damage suggests 75/25 of the creature's XP, not full XP plus bonus XP. Exact 7.6 eligibility, overkill, healing and rounding remain unverified. **Ours:** highest damage gets 100%, every other recorded attacker gets `ceil(XP/3)`; even zero damage is recorded, and overkill is added (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:368`, `:375`, `:385`). **Fix:** credit positive effective damage and split one reward proportionally; verify historical rounding/eligibility before claiming exact parity. Do not infer modern party-share bonuses for 7.6. **Confidence: medium** (official source describes later rules). [Source](https://www.tibia.com/support/?entryid=92&subtopic=gethelp).

10. **★ Protection zones and logout.** Temples, depots and houses are protection zones: offensive actions and monster entry are blocked, and PZ and logout locks are distinct. A 7.6-style server describes **60 s** fight status; PvP can additionally lock entry. **Ours:** map has no zone flags (`packages/game/src/model.ts:23`); explicit logout sets disconnected even during combat, though `present()` keeps the body in the world while its **30 s** timer remains (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:189`, `:308`, `:512`). **Fix:** add tile zones, prevent attacks by or against protected occupants and monster entry, reject voluntary logout while fight-locked, retain disconnected bodies, and separately model PvP PZ lock. **Confidence: medium** (server reconstruction; confirm exact 2005 timing). [Zones](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=combat&subtopic=manual), [old-style fight status](https://www.oldcores.com/wiki/game-mechanics/pvp-system).

11. **Magic level.** After the 7.6 update the first magic level cost **1,600 mana**, with `1600 × b^m` from magic level `m` to `m+1`; `b` was vocation-dependent (e.g. mage 1.1, knight 3). Pre-7.6 sources quote 400, so do not mix them. **Ours:** counts successful spell mana correctly but starts at 60 and scales 1.35 (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:348`, `packages/game/src/rules.ts:27`, `content/balance.json:26`). **Fix:** use 1,600 and explicitly choose a shared growth factor for the vocation-free design; deduct magic progress on death. **Confidence: high** for first threshold; medium for selecting a single multiplier. [December 2005 discussion](https://forums.tibiabr.com/threads/70671-Nova-tabela-para-Magic-Level-de-Knights-P%C3%B3s-Update/page2), [2006 formula](https://forums.tibiabr.com/threads/122048-Mana-Necess%C3%A1rio-Para-Pr%C3%B3ximo-ML).

12. **Click semantics and Look.** Classic controls: left-click walks/drag-moves; right-click attacks a creature or uses an item/container; both buttons together look; context menu can disambiguate. Later smart-click conventions should not be silently treated as 7.6. **Ours:** both clicks attack actors and most tiles use the same smart-click path, with no Look command (`apps/client/src/main.ts:258`, `apps/client/src/render/view.ts:146`, `packages/game/src/model.ts:47`). **Fix:** add classic controls plus a contextual Look that reports original item/creature descriptions and relevant stats, without copying Tibia text. **Confidence: medium** (classic guide is later but describes legacy mode). [Classic control](https://tibia.pl/strona/tutorial-classic), [2013 smart-click change](https://www.tibia.com/news/?id=2573&subtopic=newsarchive).

13. **Use, Use-with, and walking to interact.** Use opens a container or activates an object; Use-with selects a source then a destination (e.g. a tool on a tile). Approaching a distant object should complete the intended action when reachable. **Ours:** `useTile` walks toward distant doors/hatches/ladders but returns without queuing use; `useItem` only consumes food/drinks, and no use-with command exists (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:274`, `:304`, `:317`). Client separately queues corpse/vault/pile interaction (`apps/client/src/main.ts:327`). **Fix:** server-authoritative pending action with reach/revalidation/cancellation and a source-target use action. **Confidence: high** for interaction pattern; exact path/retry semantics need 7.6 testing. [Controls](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

14. **Stacking and ground containers.** Classic right-click chooses the creature over an item on the same tile; certain fixed usable objects can take priority over a bag, with a context menu to disambiguate. Ground containers open as containers, not as a list of everything on the tile. **Ours:** top movable pile item/actor/corpse are picked by separate client checks and a ground bag cannot be opened via `use()` (`apps/client/src/main.ts:263`, `:294`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:317`, `:326`). **Fix:** represent tile layers and action-specific top-target priority; open a reachable container directly, including nested ones. **Confidence: medium** (classic-control guide, exact 7.6 layer ordering needs verification). [Source](https://tibia.pl/strona/tutorial-classic).

15. **Item movement details.** Dragging can initiate auto-walk to a distant pickup; stack moves choose a quantity, and heavy objects have shorter move ranges. Items and creatures can coexist on a tile (the classic-control guide explicitly describes a creature standing over a bag). **Ours:** source must already be adjacent, client sends full stack, and `dropOnGround` allows a creature-occupied tile already; its terrain allowlist and all-item 7-tile limit are coarse (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:65`, `:100`, `:130`, `apps/client/src/main.ts:281`, `:294`, `:494`). **Fix:** preserve throwing onto occupied tiles, add queued approach, split-count UI and per-item mobility/range; verify cross-floor throw rules separately. **Confidence: medium** (exact historical ranges unverified). [Controls](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual), [tile coexistence](https://tibia.pl/strona/tutorial-classic).

16. **Doors and floor changes.** Walking onto stairs and open holes changes floors; ladders/grates use an explicit action, and a rope/tool can be required for the reverse/opening. **Ours:** hatch and ladder are both walkable, all links trigger by stepping, all doors on a path auto-open, and a remote use never finishes (`packages/game/src/model.ts:13`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:274`, `:424`). **Fix:** give each transition an explicit step/use/tool rule; don't auto-open locked doors. Whether ordinary doors auto-open in 7.6 needs a direct test. **Confidence: medium.** [2004 guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/webonly/916616-tibia/faqs/19372), [controls](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual).

17. **Pushing and following.** Drag an adjacent pushable creature/player one tile to an available destination after a delay; many large creatures are unpushable, and pushes can cost the next attack. Follow is non-attacking and ends when the target leaves the screen. **Ours:** all occupied tiles block (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:184`, `:426`); there is no push or friendly follow command (`packages/game/src/model.ts:47`). **Fix:** add validated delayed push with per-creature pushability and a separate follow target; verify precise 7.6 push timing. **Confidence: medium** (modern manual plus 7.7-era server discussion). [Controls](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=controls&subtopic=manual), [7.7 push flags](https://otland.net/threads/7-7-realots-7-7-cipsoft-files-virgin.244562/page-58).

18. **Keyboard and map pathing.** Arrow keys and keypad directions include diagonals; 7.x-era clients route using locally known map data, rather than an omniscient server route. A later OTClient limits a requested route to **127 steps**, but this is **not verified as a 7.6 client limit**. **Ours:** arrows/WASD combine for diagonals already, but sends on a fixed 260 ms cadence; server A* searches up to 4,000 nodes across the whole map (`apps/client/src/main.ts:337`, `packages/game/src/world.ts:23`). **Fix:** retain arrow diagonals, synchronize repeat with actual step readiness, add keypad support and client-known-map/path limits; investigate the historical cap before setting it. **Confidence: low** for exact 7.6 limits. [Later OTClient implementation](https://git.someserver.de/gesche/tibia-client/commit/a1a8d28f5cc3e3ee73258bebca74a1a9cc13adb4?style=unified&whitespace=show-all), [keypad recollection](https://www.reddit.com/r/TibiaMMO/comments/nv2ze2).

19. **Spell and item exhaustion.** Contemporary reconstructions disagree on interaction: a common 7.4–7.6 account gives **2 s offensive, 1 s healing/support, roughly 1 s fluids**; another describes distinct item-use and spell exhaustion with asymmetric fluid interaction. Modern independent spell groups should not be assumed. **Ours:** one `spellAt`; a drink is allowed during the final 500 ms of `spellAt`, then sets `spellAt` to now+1 s (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:317`, `:329`). **Fix:** model action and spell timing explicitly and validate an attack→drink→heal sequence against a 7.6 reference. **Confidence: low** on exact interactions. [7.4–7.6 discussion](https://otland.net/threads/real-exhaust-formulas-from-7-4-7-6.244452/), [alternative reconstruction](https://otland.net/threads/old-exhaust-system.249822/).

20. **Monster behavior and speed.** The 7.4 reconstruction has per-creature speed, low-HP flee thresholds, and target-switch chances/strategies (nearest, low HP, highest damage or random); cadence is uncertain. Speed uses the same terrain formula as players. **Ours:** fixed `def.step` regardless of terrain/diagonal, always pursues current visible target, no flee or switch fields (`packages/game/src/content-schema.ts:56`, `packages/game/src/engine.ts:498`, `:516`). **Fix:** add original per-species speed/flee/switch parameters, and run monster steps through the terrain timing rule without changing the already-implemented anti-lure/respawn behavior. **Confidence: medium** (7.4/nearby-version evidence). [Creature behavior](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/Creature), [speed](https://tibiantis-notes.github.io/speed).

21. **Capacity and light.** Capacity is total carried weight including nested containers; 7.x no-vocation gain is +10 capacity/level. Darkness and carried/temporary light visibly change exploration. **Ours:** recursive weight/capacity checks already exist, but use 220 +20/level (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:55`, `:63`, `:79`, `content/balance.json:7`); renderer supplies an automatic cave torch (`apps/client/src/render/view.ts:478`) with no item light state. **Fix:** retain recursive weight, choose baseline/gain explicitly, and add an independently designed light radius/duration system. **Confidence: medium** (historical details of exact light levels not checked). [Capacity gains](https://tibiaibz.wordpress.com/tibia-ibiza/guias-y-tutoriales/hacer-un-server-desde-0-tibia-76/), [controls/manual](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=characters&subtopic=manual).

22. **PvP, one line.** If PvP is added, old-style skulls mark unjustified aggression and repeated kills can produce a red skull with full item loss; Lanternfall currently only targets monsters (`packages/game/src/engine.ts:270`); add this only as part of PvP. **Confidence: medium.** [Source](https://www.oldcores.com/wiki/game-mechanics/pvp-system).

**Already aligned, do not 'fix':** XP thresholds in `packages/game/src/rules.ts:10` with `[50,-150,400]` in `content/balance.json:9` are algebraically the standard curve: level 2 = 100, level 8 = 4,200, level 10 = 9,300. [Formula](https://tibiamobile.com/tools/experience-calculator/). Arrow diagonals, 7-tile throw, adjacent pickup, ground-item stacking, screen luring, respawn blocking/jitter and corpse stages are also already present; this report does not assert their precise historical implementation is proven.

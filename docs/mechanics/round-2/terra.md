# Round 2 review

**Score: 6/10.** The main formulas and proportional XP are present, but protection-zone enforcement, movement timing and stand mode have player-visible failures. `npm test` passes all 47 tests. Targeted engine reproductions confirmed the PZ attack, stand-mode, timing and pile-limit failures; the remaining findings follow from code inspection.

## Blocking issues

1. **An attack can be made from inside a protection zone.** `packages/game/src/engine.ts:432` checks the zone before moving, but the same tick moves the player at `:445` and attacks at `:459`. I placed a player at the shrine entrance with an adjacent target, walked onto the protected door tile, and observed a melee swing and damage while `geo.pz(p)` was true. The target remains set until the next tick, so `cast()` at `:344` also permits an attack spell during that interval. **Fix:** clear/validate the target after movement and immediately before every melee hit, and reject offensive casts inside `cast()` whenever the caster is in a PZ. Add a test that enters a PZ with a live target and attempts both attacks before the next tick.

2. **Stand mode continues an existing chase route.** `packages/game/src/engine.ts:312` changes `p.chase` but leaves the previously generated path intact; `:439` executes that path regardless of mode. In a reproduction the player moved three more tiles after switching to stand. **Fix:** when switching chase off, clear an active chase-generated path (`!p.manual`) while preserving an explicitly requested manual path and the target. Also clear chase-generated paths when the target is cancelled or dies, so the old pursuit cannot continue.

3. **The 50 ms step formula is quantized again to 100 ms.** `packages/game/src/engine.ts:439` only executes steps on a tick, and `apps/server/src/index.ts:40` schedules ticks every 100 ms with `engine.tick()` at `:62`. A level-3 player on plank has a computed 450 ms step, but consecutive steps occur 500 ms apart; every duration ending in 50 ms incurs the same extra delay. **Fix:** run the simulation at 50 ms and call `engine.tick(50)`, or process movement deadlines at 50 ms resolution; preserve the intended wall-clock persistence cadence. Test actual consecutive step timestamps, not just the value stored in `moveAt`.

4. **Diagonal animation takes longer than the computed step.** `apps/client/src/render/view.ts:327` computes the correct diagonal duration, but `:435`–`:436` move at one world unit per duration while a diagonal covers √2 world units. A diagonal therefore takes about 1.35 times its scheduled duration, so the visual actor falls behind subsequent authoritative steps; cardinal movement is also shortened by the `1.05` multiplier. **Fix:** interpolate from the recorded start position to the destination using elapsed time divided by the actual step duration, clamped to 1, for both cardinal and diagonal steps.

5. **A rejected logout still disconnects the client.** `apps/client/src/main.ts:399` relies on a potentially stale view, while `apps/client/src/net.ts:39` sends `logout` and unconditionally leaves 150 ms later. If a fight starts after the last view, the server rejects the action at `packages/game/src/engine.ts:310`, but the client still closes and reloads; the character remains exposed as a disconnected combatant. **Fix:** correlate the logout request with its server result and only leave/reload on success; on rejection keep the session open and show the error. Keep forced-disconnect body retention separate.

6. **A full ground pile can grow beyond its limit.** `packages/game/src/engine.ts:144` accepts a full pile whenever any matching stack has room, even if that room cannot absorb the whole incoming stack; `:147`–`:149` create the remainder as another pile entry. With 19 items and a 99-gold stack on a 20-item tile, dropping 100 gold produces 21 entries (100 and 99 gold). Repeating this can keep growing the pile. **Fix:** calculate available space in existing matching stacks before moving; on a full pile move at most that amount and leave the remainder at the source, or reject the entire move. Only create a new ground stack if a tile slot remains, including split and same-tile cases.

## Non-blocking notes

1. **Progression rounding drifts from the stated formulas:** `packages/game/src/rules.ts:11` rounds `50 * 1.1` up to 56 because JavaScript represents it as `55.00000000000001`; melee 12 therefore starts at 106 rather than 105 tries. `:32` and `:45`–`:46` compound previously rounded magic costs: the cost at magic level 4 is 6148 instead of `ceil(1600 * 1.4^4) = 6147`. Calculate each threshold from the base and exponent with stable decimal/integer rounding, and share that calculation between the display and cumulative thresholds.
2. **Weapon defense has no effect with shipped equipment:** the fallback in `packages/game/src/engine.ts:568`–`:570` is sound, but none of the four weapon entries in `content/balance.json:70`–`:104` has a `defense` value. Give weapons intentional defense values and verify an unshielded player uses melee skill and consumes a block opportunity without training shielding.
3. **Look can describe an arbitrary actor regardless of location:** `packages/game/src/engine.ts:499`–`:505` resolves the supplied ID before the range check at `:507`, without confirming that the actor is present at `at` or even on the same floor. Validate the actor's current position, visibility and range before describing it.
4. **Accepted PZ logout leaves a visible body:** `packages/game/src/engine.ts:190` treats every disconnected player with a future combat timer as present, including someone who successfully logged out in a PZ at `:310`–`:311`. Distinguish an accepted voluntary logout from a dropped connection so the former disappears while preserving any remaining fight timer for a later reconnect.
5. **The new block/PZ tests do not establish their names' claims:** `tests/engine.test.ts:321` permits four shielding tries in 2 seconds, and `:356`–`:364` never makes a monster attempt PZ entry or target a protected player. Assert exactly two eligible opportunities with controlled attackers, then verify no third shield roll/training; exercise monster approach and targeting at the actual PZ boundary.

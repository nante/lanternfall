# Lanternfall playtest, 10 November 2026

How each item was found:
- **Played**: seen in the running game at `localhost:4173` against the live server, with two heroes in two tabs.
- **Ran**: reproduced with a script against `new Engine(buildTown(), defaultBalance)` (scripts in the session scratchpad, `engine-audit/c1.ts` to `c4.ts`).
- **Read**: found by reading the code and not reproduced.

Counts: 63 bugs (16 played, 15 ran, 32 read) and 11 missing basics a Tibia player expects in the first ten minutes.

## H. Missing basics a Tibia player expects

1. Right-click context menu: Look, Use, Open, Attack, Follow, Trade with. Look exists only as Shift+click on the world, and not at all on inventory or loot.
2. Push a creature, player or NPC to a neighbouring tile.
3. Move floor items from a distance (auto-walk to the source), and drag between any two tiles in reach.
4. Stack split or a count prompt when dragging a stack.
5. More than one container window; bags as their own windows.
6. Use-with (a potion or rune on a target or the floor), and using items that lie on the ground.
7. Follow mode, and targeting or following players.
8. Chat basics beyond local speech: bubbles, yell and whisper, private messages, channels, history recall with the up arrow.
9. Configurable hotkeys; the 1-7 bar is fixed.
10. Esc to stop walking; numpad and single-key diagonals.
11. Player-to-player trade.

## Checked and working

Equip and unequip by drag, wrong-slot refusal, pack swaps, HUD drags not moving the hero, typing in chat not moving the hero, minimap click-to-walk, hatch and ladder, buying, spell hotkey, NPC dialogue chips, two heroes stepping to one tile in the same tick, selling equipped items refused, exact-gold purchase, throws off the map refused, zero and negative counts refused, stale item versions refused, attacks from a protection zone blocked.

## Not covered

Death and respawn in play (the audit covered the rules), the vault, the smith, inn and apothecary, spells beyond Mend, the Vista, play on a real network with latency, and anything with darkness turned on in play.
